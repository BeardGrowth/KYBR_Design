KYBR · WEBSITE INQUIRIES

How your inquiry is handled

This notice describes information collected by the demonstration request form on this website. It is not a full privacy policy for future KYBR software products. A comprehensive policy should be approved before public production launch.

Information you choose to provide

The KYBR inquiry form asks for your name, work email, business name, approximate number of business locations, primary business priority, and any optional details you provide. The form may also record which KYBR service option you selected.

Why we request it

We intend to use inquiry information to review your request, communicate with you about KYBR and discuss possible services or demonstrations. Submitting an inquiry does not purchase a service.

Form processing

The website is configured to use Netlify Forms to collect inquiries. If that service is enabled for this deployment, Netlify processes form submissions for the site owner. We have not independently verified the live form configuration, storage settings, retention period, notification recipients or any additional integrations. Those details must be confirmed before accepting production leads.

Please avoid sensitive information

Do not include passwords, payment card information, patient or medical records, or other sensitive personal information in your message. The form is intended only for ordinary business inquiries.

Data access, retention and requests

Access and retention practices depend on the production hosting and lead-management configuration. Before launch, KYBR must confirm who can access submissions, how long they are retained, and how information requests are handled. For inquiries about information you submitted, email hello@kybrmarketing.ai.

Third-party content

Some pages may load external fonts or illustrative media. Their providers may receive technical request information when those resources are loaded. We plan to reduce unnecessary external dependencies before public launch.

Status: Website inquiry disclosure for a staging preview. Legal review and production-specific details remain outstanding.
