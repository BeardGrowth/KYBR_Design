# KYBR Search & Conversion Readiness — 2026-11-10

## Staging guardrails
The Netlify preview is deliberately marked **noindex** in `robots.txt` and `_headers`. Do not remove these until the public production domain has been selected and approved.

## Production SEO launch checklist
1. Verify exact product/module readiness and replace aspirational claims with supported demonstrations.
2. Choose the canonical domain (likely kybrmarketing.ai), implement HTTPS redirects, canonical tags with actual production URLs, and unique page titles.
3. Remove preview-wide X-Robots-Tag and robots disallow only for approved indexable production pages. Keep thank-you and internal demo pages noindex as needed.
4. Create a production sitemap.xml and submit verified property and sitemap in Google Search Console.
5. Add validated Organization and SoftwareApplication schema only with accurate legal name, URLs, plans, support information and verified feature details. Do not invent ratings, reviews, prices, customers or results.
6. Collect real case studies, screenshots, author bios and customer-approved proof. Publish a privacy policy, data retention and upload-consent documentation and accessible support contact.
7. Confirm forms in Netlify Forms UI and set up notification routing / CRM integration; test submissions end to end and monitor for spam.
8. Check mobile UX, Core Web Vitals, keyboard navigation, contrast, semantic heading hierarchy, image alt text and link functionality.
9. Build content clustered around AI marketing automation, marketing operations software, CRM and outreach workflows, enterprise multi-location marketing and brand-aware content.
10. Configure analytics and track qualified demo submissions, product-tour engagement and funnel progression without collecting unnecessary personal data.
