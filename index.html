COMMON QUESTIONS

Powerful behind the scenes. Simple to understand.

Is KYBR software, an agency, or both?

KYBR is a technology platform with optional managed marketing and implementation services. Which tools and services you receive depends on the plan and agreement.

What can I manage in the dashboard?

KYBR's intended system brings content, marketing, customer records, tasks, outreach, campaign activity and reporting together. We confirm which modules are available and production-ready in a demonstration.

Does AI publish things without approval?

Publishing and other consequential actions should follow your configured permissions and approval rules. Initial campaign activation is designed to include a human review stage.

Do we need to replace our existing software?

Not necessarily. KYBR is designed to work with selected external systems when appropriate. Specific integrations must be assessed and verified for your business.

How does the first month work?

Our intended onboarding process gathers business context, configures approved workflows, tests brand output and establishes an execution and reporting routine. Exact scope is agreed with your team.
