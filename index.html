BEFORE YOU DECIDE

Good questions.
Clear answers.

You should understand the software and the service before choosing either.

Is KYBR software or a marketing agency?

Both options are available in the proposed KYBR offering. You can discuss a platform-led engagement for your own team or a managed service with defined execution responsibilities. Exact availability and deliverables are confirmed in your proposal.

Do we have to replace our existing CRM or marketing tools?

No automatic replacement is required. We'll review your current stack and identify which integrations or migrations, if any, are practical and supported.

What happens in the first 30 days?

Our planned onboarding covers brand and goal discovery, configuration of approved workflows, testing, team training and the initial operating rhythm. A written agreement will specify the actual timeline and deliverables.

Can I approve content before it goes live?

Content approvals are part of the intended workflow. Specific permission controls and publishing integrations are reviewed during your demo and verified for your plan.

How much does KYBR cost?

We offer a scoped consultation rather than displaying unconfirmed pricing. Costs depend on platform access, location count, onboarding, integrations and whether managed services are included. You'll receive the scope and pricing before making a commitment.

Can KYBR support multiple brands or locations?

Multi-location operations are a core design direction. We'll confirm the roles, reporting, permissions and integrations supported today, and outline any additional implementation requirements before you purchase.
